# Sahakar Sathi - Features and Innovations

1. **Bio-Economic Matching Engine**
   - Multi-factor scoring system integrating worker equity (82%) and logistical efficiency (18%).
   - Explainable matching: Transparent scoring breakdown and human-readable matching explanations.
   - Fatigue cap: Strict physiological workload limit (80% maximum fatigue threshold) excluding exhausted workers from dispatch.

2. **Bayesian Trust Score**
   - Weighted reputation scoring incorporating review history, punctuality, reliability, tenure, and completion records with exponential decay for older feedback.

3. **Privacy-Preserving Proof of Work**
   - On-device hash generation with server-side Hamming distance replay detection to prevent duplicate submissions without storing photos.

4. **Fuzzy Phonetic Voice Assistant**
   - Voice confirmation interface with phonetic matching supporting Hindi and English voice commands, with strict negative confirmation priority.

5. **Cooperative Governance**
   - 1-member-1-vote governance model ensuring democratic control and equitable policy participation across all cooperative members.

6. **Multi-Channel Access**
   - Multi-channel platform access supporting Web, WhatsApp, and Voice interfaces for worker and customer accessibility.

7. **Simulated Equity & Forecasting**
   - Monte Carlo simulation engine modeling earnings distributions and Gini inequality under alternative allocation strategies.

8. **Worker Peer Verification**
   - Community-driven peer vouching network enabling verified cooperative members to vouch for new candidate workers.
