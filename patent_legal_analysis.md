# Sahakar Sathi — Patent Acceptance Analysis (India)

> **Legal Framework:** Patents Act 1970, Section 3(k) + CRI Guidelines 2025 (July 29, 2025)  
> **Key Rule:** India does NOT allow patents on "a computer programme per se" or "business methods"

> **Good News (2025 update):** Novel hardware is NOT required anymore — pure software can be patented IF it solves a technical problem

### PATENT 1: Privacy-Preserving Edge AI Proof-of-Work

#### ✅ Why It Should Be Accepted
- **Clear technical effect:** On-device image processing, perceptual hash computation, entropy-based privacy scoring, and data destruction — these are all measurable technical operations
- **Solves a real technical problem:** How to verify service completion without transmitting privacy-sensitive imagery
- **Hardware interaction:** Uses device camera, HTML5 Canvas API, client-side computation
- **2025 Guidelines favorable:** AI/ML-based verification systems are specifically mentioned as patentable if they produce a technical effect

#### ⚠️ Loopholes & Risks
1. **Prior Art Risk (HIGH):** Perceptual hashing (aHash, pHash, dHash) algorithms have been publicly documented since the 2000s. The examiner may argue your hash computation is not novel
2. **Implementation vs. Invention:** The IPO may argue you're simply applying existing hashing techniques to a new use case (service verification), which is an "obvious" combination
3. **"Zero-knowledge" claim is misleading:** True privacy-preserving on-device verification proofs have a specific cryptographic definition (ZK-SNARKs, etc.). Your system is "privacy-preserving" but not technically "privacy-preserving on-device verification" in the cryptographic sense

#### 🔧 How to Fix & Strengthen
- **DO NOT claim** "perceptual hashing" as your invention — claim the **complete pipeline**: capture → downscale → hash → entropy score → pixel destruction → anti-replay verification
- **Emphasize the privacy score computation** — Shannon entropy calculation for privacy grading (A-D) is a novel scoring method
- **Emphasize the anti-replay mechanism** — checking hash uniqueness across booking history to prevent fraud
- **Reword claim as:** *"A system and method for privacy-preserving verification of physical service completion comprising on-device perceptual hashing with automated source data destruction and cross-transaction hash uniqueness validation"*

#### 📊 Verdict: 🟢 STRONG (75-85% chance)

### PATENT 2: 7-Dimensional Composite Trust Score

#### ✅ Why It Should Be Accepted
- **Multi-dimensional computation** with weighted scoring is technically sophisticated
- **Novel dimensions:** Proof Compliance (% of jobs with valid edge AI hash) and Economic Equity (from the 5D equity index) don't exist in any known trust scoring system
- **Cascading system integration:** Trust score feeds into micro-credit limits, governance voting weight, and peer verification authority — this is a genuine technical architecture

#### ⚠️ Loopholes & Risks
1. **Section 3(k) "Mathematical Method" risk (HIGH):** A weighted average of 7 numbers is fundamentally a mathematical formula. The examiner will likely challenge this
2. **Prior Art:** Multi-factor reputation scoring exists in Uber (driver ratings), Airbnb (Superhost), and academic literature
3. **"Composite score" is common:** Many systems combine multiple factors into a single score — this alone is not novel

#### 🔧 How to Fix & Strengthen
- **DO NOT claim** the weighted average formula — claim the **integration architecture**
- **Focus on the novel dimensions** that don't exist elsewhere: Proof Compliance (integration with edge AI hashing), Economic Equity (integration with equity index), and Tenure
- **Claim the cascading privilege system:** Score → Tier → Micro-credit limits + Vote weight + Peer authority
- **Reword claim as:** *"A computer-implemented system for generating a multi-dimensional worker trust assessment in cooperative service platforms, integrating real-time proof-of-work compliance verification and economic equity metrics, wherein said trust assessment dynamically controls financial credit limits, governance voting weights, and peer verification authority"*

#### 📊 Verdict: 🟡 MEDIUM (50-60% chance)

### PATENT 3: 5-Dimensional Workload Equity Index

#### ✅ Why It Should Be Accepted
- **Clear technical effect:** Worker safety through fatigue-based automatic exclusion from job matching
- **Job-type intensity mapping** (plumbing: 0.85, carpentry: 0.80, etc.) is a genuinely novel data model
- **Geographic burden tracking** across assignment history involves actual geospatial computation (Haversine distance)
- **Temporal fairness monitoring** involves time-based analysis
- **Inequality index** (standard deviation of composite scores) is a cooperative-wide analytics feature

#### ⚠️ Loopholes & Risks
1. **Fatigue monitoring prior art:** Industrial fatigue monitoring has 295+ patents (mostly in mining/transportation) — but these use wearable sensors, not algorithmic estimation
2. **"Equity scoring" may be seen as business method:** Fair job allocation is a business objective, not a technical one
3. **Simple formulas:** Income parity deficit = target - actual earnings. This is basic subtraction

#### 🔧 How to Fix & Strengthen
- **Emphasize the SAFETY angle:** Automatic worker exclusion when fatigue capacity ≤ 10.0 is a safety system, not a business method. Safety systems have strong patent precedent
- **Emphasize the technical computation:** Haversine distance for geographic burden, job-type intensity coefficients, temporal slot classification
- **Distinguish from industrial fatigue:** Your system uses NO wearable sensors — it's a purely algorithmic inference model based on work history patterns, which is a different approach
- **Reword claim as:** *"A computer-implemented safety system for preventing worker fatigue-related incidents in service allocation platforms, comprising a multi-dimensional workload equity assessment using job-type physical intensity coefficients, geospatial burden tracking via Haversine distance computation, and automatic worker exclusion when computed fatigue capacity falls below a safety threshold"*

#### 📊 Verdict: 🟢 STRONG (70-80% chance)

#### ⚠️ Loopholes & Risks

3. **Your implementation is simpler** than true reputation-weighted quorum protocols — you don't handle network partitions, message ordering, or asynchronous faults

#### 🔧 How to Fix & Strengthen

- **Emphasize the hybrid model:** Centralized (Gov ID + criminal check) → Decentralized (peer quorum) is a genuinely novel two-phase pipeline
- **Emphasize the anti-gaming features:** Trust-score-gated voting access (only 70+ can vote), preventing self-voting, weight caps at 3.0×

### PATENT 7: Democratic Governance with Weighted Voting

#### ⚠️ Loopholes & Risks (CRITICAL)

> [!CAUTION]
> This patent has the HIGHEST rejection risk under Indian law.

1. **Section 3(k) "Business Method" — HARD BLOCK:** Democratic governance of a cooperative is fundamentally a business/organizational method. The Indian Patent Office has consistently rejected business method patents, even when implemented via software
2. **Voting systems are prior art:** Weighted voting, supermajority rules, and proposal lifecycle management are well-established in cooperative law, corporate governance, and DAOs (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations)
3. **SHA-256 hashing of votes is standard:** Tamper-proof audit trails using cryptographic hashing are not novel
4. **No technical problem being solved:** The system solves an organizational/governance problem, not a technical one

#### 🔧 Can It Be Saved?
- **Very difficult.** The 2025 Guidelines explicitly state that business methods are an "absolute bar" even if implemented using technical means
- **Only possibility:** Reframe as a "tamper-evident distributed voting protocol" focusing on the cryptographic integrity mechanism, NOT the governance aspect
- **Merge with Patent 6** instead — the weighted voting mechanism can be claimed as part of the peer verification consensus protocol

#### 📊 Verdict: 🔴 WEAK (25-35% chance) — recommend NOT filing separately

### PATENT 8: Phonetic Fuzzy Intent Matching for Low-Literacy Voice Control

#### ✅ Why It Should Be Accepted
- **Clear technical effect:** Natural language processing for Hindi-English code-switching speech
- **Novel phonetic normalization:** Soundex-style mapping specifically designed for Indian languages (sh→s, ee→i, oo→u, v→w, ph→f, z→j) is genuinely new
- **Levenshtein distance** applied to phonetically normalized Hindi tokens is a novel NLP technique
- **Accessibility angle:** Designed for low-literacy users — this is a genuine technical problem (how to control software without reading)
- **Multi-language support:** Hindi, Marathi, English with cross-lingual phoneme mapping

#### ⚠️ Loopholes & Risks
1. **Levenshtein distance is prior art:** The algorithm itself is from 1965
2. **Soundex is prior art:** The original Soundex algorithm is from 1918
3. **Speech recognition APIs are not yours:** You're using the browser's built-in SpeechRecognition API
4. **Limited scope:** Only 4 intents (earnings, jobs, accept, start) — examiner may question the "inventive step" for such a narrow application

#### 🔧 How to Fix & Strengthen
- **DO NOT claim** Levenshtein or Soundex as inventions — claim the **Hindi-English phonetic normalization rules** and the **cross-lingual fuzzy matching pipeline**
- **Expand the intent vocabulary** to demonstrate broader applicability
- **Document the phoneme groupings** as a novel linguistic contribution
- **Reword claim as:** *"A natural language processing method for cross-lingual intent recognition in Hindi-English code-switching speech, comprising a phonetic normalization module with language-specific phoneme grouping rules for Indian languages, a fuzzy string matching module using edit distance on normalized tokens, and a confidence-based intent classification module with haptic feedback confirmation"*

#### 📊 Verdict: 🟢 STRONG (70-80% chance)
